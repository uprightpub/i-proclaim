Responsible publishing

Publishing Policies

Principles supporting fair review, scholarly integrity and a trustworthy academic record.

Publication Ethics

Authors, editors and reviewers are expected to uphold recognized standards of originality, authorship, disclosure, confidentiality and responsible scholarly conduct.

Research Integrity

Submissions should report research honestly and accurately, with appropriate approvals, transparent methods and reliable records supporting the findings.

Peer Review

Manuscripts are assessed for relevance, quality and scholarly contribution through an impartial editorial and peer-review process appropriate to each journal.

Corrections and Retractions

Material errors and concerns are evaluated carefully. Corrections, expressions of concern or retractions may be issued when necessary to protect the scholarly record.
