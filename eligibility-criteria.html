General conditions

Who may be nominated?

◎

Global participation

Applications are welcomed from eligible individuals working in academic, research, professional, nonprofit, corporate or independent settings.

↗

Self or third-party nomination

An eligible individual may apply directly, or a professional may nominate them with their knowledge and accurate evidence.

✓

Verifiable information

Names, affiliations, publications, metrics and supporting claims must be accurate, accessible and capable of independent verification.

Submitting an application does not guarantee an award. i-Proclaim may request further evidence, reject incomplete or misleading entries, and withdraw recognition where material information is later found to be inaccurate.

Publication Excellence

Publication Citation

Career-stage categories

Where a specific Research Meet announces age-based recognition, the category is determined by age on the published cut-off date for that event.

Lifetime Achievement55 years and above
Distinguished Scientist45–54 years
Outstanding Scientist35–44 years
Young Scientist25–34 years
These career-stage labels apply only when listed in a particular event announcement. The three core award programmes remain governed by their own published criteria.
