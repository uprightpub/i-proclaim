What partnership means

Specialist support without losing your institutional identity

The partner organization retains its name, mission, governance and scholarly responsibility. i‑Proclaim provides agreed publishing, technology and operational support behind those activities.

◉
Partnership means working together under a documented scope to strengthen the organization’s own scholarly programmes, not replacing its academic authority.

A collaboration may cover one journal, one conference, a digital repository, a series of publications or a longer-term portfolio of scholarly activities.

YOU LEAD

Academic ownership

The organization determines its mission, academic direction, appointments and final scholarly decisions.

WE SUPPORT

Publishing operations

We provide the agreed systems, documentation, workflow assistance, production and technical support.

TOGETHER

Development planning

Partnership pathway

From first discussion to accountable delivery

No two organizations have exactly the same structure, so every collaboration begins with discovery and documented expectations.

  1. 01

    Explore

    Understand the organization, programme, authority, audience and priorities.

  2. 02

    Assess

    Review governance, people, systems, content, risks and available capacity.

  3. 03

    Define

    Agree scope, responsibilities, deliverables, costs, timeline and reporting.

  4. 04

    Implement

    Build or support the programme with testing, documentation and communication.

  5. 05

    Review

    Evaluate delivery, resolve gaps and set realistic next-stage priorities.

Partnership principles

Built for credible, sustainable scholarship

Collaboration is accepted only where the proposed activity can be delivered transparently and without compromising the scholarly record.

01

Identity preserved

The partner’s ownership and institutional identity remain visible and accurately represented.

02

Roles documented

Academic decisions and service responsibilities are clearly separated.

03

Integrity protected

No fabricated review, authorship, affiliation, indexing status or misleading evidence.

04

Capacity strengthened

Documentation and knowledge transfer support long-term internal capability.

05

Outcomes represented honestly

No guarantee of indexing, citations, rankings, accreditation or regulatory recognition.
