Minimum eligibility

Meet all three indicators

Any researcher may apply when their publicly accessible Google Scholar profile shows at least the figures below at the time of submission.

500minimum citations
5minimum h-index
10minimum i10-index
All three thresholds are required. Meeting the figures makes an application eligible for verification; it does not bypass identity, authenticity or profile checks.
