Metadata and interoperability

Help reliable records travel further

Repository visibility begins with accessible pages and accurate, consistent metadata. We configure supported discovery components while preserving the institution’s responsibility for record quality and rights management.

  • OAI-PMH repository identification and metadata exposure
  • Meaningful titles, authors, dates, abstracts and document types
  • Institutional subjects, divisions and browsing structures
  • Persistent identifier and ORCID-related integration where applicable
  • Search-engine accessibility and technical indexing readiness
  • Usage statistics or analytics integration where supported

Configuration can improve technical readiness, but inclusion, ranking, indexing speed and citation outcomes remain decisions of external services and are not guaranteed.
