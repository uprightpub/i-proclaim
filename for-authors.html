Journal guidance

For Authors

Essential information to help authors prepare, submit and follow their scholarly work.

Manuscript Requirements

Prepare an original, clearly structured manuscript that follows the scope, formatting, citation and ethical requirements of the selected journal. Journal-specific instructions will be added here.

Publication Frequency

Publication schedules vary by journal. Please consult the relevant journal portal for its current issue frequency and submission timetable.

Publication Charges

Any applicable editorial or publication charge will be communicated transparently before publication. Current journal-specific information will be listed here.

Apply to Be a Reviewer

Qualified researchers and subject specialists are welcome to express interest in peer-review service. Application details and eligibility guidance will be published here.
