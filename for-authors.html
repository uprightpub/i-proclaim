01

Manuscript Requirements

Authors should prepare an original, complete and clearly written manuscript that fits the aims and scope of the selected journal. The following general requirements apply unless a journal provides additional instructions.

File and length

Submit the manuscript as an editable Microsoft Word file. A standard research article should normally contain approximately 3,000–6,000 words, including references and appendices.

Title and abstract

Use a concise and informative title. Include an abstract of up to 300 words stating the purpose, approach, principal findings and contribution, followed by up to seven keywords.

Author information

Provide each author’s full name, institutional affiliation and email address, and identify the corresponding author. Funding, acknowledgements and relevant conflicts of interest must be disclosed.

Structure and presentation

Organise the article with clear headings. Tables and figures must be numbered, cited in the text, supplied at publishable quality and accompanied by informative titles or captions.

References

References must be complete, accurate and consistent. Follow the style specified by the selected journal and include DOI or stable URL information where available.

Ethics and originality

The work must not be published or under consideration elsewhere. Required ethical approval, informed consent, data permissions and declarations must be included where applicable.

Before submission: Consult the individual journal website because subject-specific or article-type requirements may vary.

02

Publication Frequency

Until 2023, i-Proclaim journals normally published two scheduled issues each year. From 2024 onward, the journals follow a continuous or rolling publication model.

Each journal now publishes one volume and one issue per calendar year. After an article has successfully completed editorial assessment, peer review, revision and production, it may be published online within the current annual issue without waiting for a fixed half-yearly release date.

Submission and peer review remain open throughout the year. Publication timing depends on completion of the editorial and production process.

03

Publication Charges

i-Proclaim journals apply an Article Processing Charge (APC) only to manuscripts accepted for publication. Submission and peer-review decisions are based on editorial merit and are independent of an author’s ability to pay.

The above rate currently applies to all i-Proclaim journals. Any bank, transfer or currency-conversion cost charged by a payment provider is the responsibility of the payer.

Important: Payment is requested only after formal acceptance. An APC does not guarantee acceptance and does not influence peer review or the editorial decision.

04

Apply to Be a Reviewer

i-Proclaim welcomes applications from active researchers, academics and subject specialists who can provide timely, constructive and impartial peer review. Applicants should normally hold a postgraduate qualification, with preference given to PhD holders or candidates who have a relevant publication record.

Reviewer responsibilities

  • Accept assignments only within their area of expertise and available time.
  • Declare any actual or potential conflict of interest promptly.
  • Treat manuscripts and review correspondence as confidential.
  • Provide specific, evidence-based and respectful comments.
  • Assess originality, methodology, clarity, relevance and support for the conclusions.

Interested applicants may review the detailed referee guidance and application information through the journal portal.
