Privacy Policy

This policy explains how personal information is handled when you visit the i-Proclaim website, contact us or interact with our academic publishing and research-development services.

PrivacyLegalCookies

Effective date: 3 September 2026

1. Scope and Responsibility

This Privacy Policy applies to the website operated by i-Proclaim Research Academy, a Malaysia-based independent platform supporting academic publishing, research development and scholarly collaboration. References to “i-Proclaim”, “we”, “our” or “us” relate to the platform responsible for the information described in this policy.

2. Information We May Receive

We collect only information reasonably connected with operating the website, responding to enquiries and providing requested services. This may include:

  • your name, email address, telephone number, institution and country;
  • information included in an enquiry, application, manuscript, reviewer CV or other material you voluntarily send;
  • correspondence and administrative records related to submissions, services or payments;
  • basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, referring page, date and time, device information and server-security logs; and
  • information received through external platforms when you choose to interact with a linked journal portal, video, form, email or other service.

Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for a clearly identified purpose and you are authorised to provide it.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • respond to questions and communicate about requested services;
  • administer manuscript submissions, editorial assessment and peer review;
  • review applications from authors, reviewers, editors, partners or award participants;
  • process legitimate administrative and payment records;
  • maintain website security, prevent misuse and diagnose technical problems;
  • preserve appropriate scholarly and business records; and
  • comply with applicable legal, regulatory, contractual or ethical obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use information submitted for editorial or service purposes for unrelated advertising.

4. When Information May Be Shared

Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary with authorised editors, reviewers, publishing partners, technical providers, payment providers or professional advisers involved in the relevant activity. We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect rights or security, or to investigate credible concerns involving fraud, misconduct or misuse.

Manuscripts and peer-review materials are handled according to the confidentiality rules described in our Peer Review Policy. Reviewers receive only the information needed for the applicable review model.

5. External Services and International Processing

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages and links to external journal, video, email and information services. Information processed by those services is governed by their own privacy terms. Because online service providers may operate in different countries, technical or submitted information may be processed outside your country. We take reasonable steps to use reputable providers and limit information to what is needed.

6. Retention and Security

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was received, legitimate record-keeping, dispute resolution, scholarly-record preservation, or legal and contractual obligations. Retention periods may differ for enquiries, financial records, rejected submissions, peer-review records and published articles.

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information. However, no email, website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices and Requests

Subject to applicable law and legitimate publishing or record-keeping requirements, you may ask us to access, correct or update personal information associated with you, or to consider a request concerning its use or deletion. We may need to verify your identity before acting. Certain records may need to be retained to protect the integrity of the scholarly record or meet legal obligations.

8. Children’s Privacy

The website and services are intended for researchers, authors, professionals and adult users. They are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly request personal information from children through this website.

9. Policy Updates

We may revise this policy when our website, services or legal obligations change. The current version and effective date will remain available on this page.
