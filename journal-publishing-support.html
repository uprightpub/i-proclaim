Journal development

From publishing idea to sustainable scholarly operation

A journal needs more than a website. It requires a clear identity, accountable governance, consistent policies, workable editorial procedures and a realistic development plan.

◫

A tailored development framework

We assess the journal’s purpose, subject coverage, ownership, audience and present publishing capacity. The resulting plan identifies priorities and practical steps for building reliable editorial and publication practices.

  • Journal identity, aims, scope and publishing model
  • Editorial structure, responsibilities and decision pathways
  • Publication policies, author guidance and ethical safeguards
  • Discoverability, metadata quality and long-term development

OJS / PKP implementation

Technical infrastructure designed around the journal

We provide implementation and operational support using Open Journal Systems (OJS), the open-source journal management and publishing platform developed by the Public Knowledge Project (PKP).

Establishment pathway

A practical route from concept to publication

The exact sequence depends on whether the project is a new journal, a relaunch, or an existing OJS publication requiring development.

  1. 01

    Discovery & assessment

    Review objectives, ownership, discipline, audience, resources and existing infrastructure.

  2. 02

    Journal framework

    Develop identity, scope, governance, policies, editorial structure and publication plan.

  3. 03

    Platform implementation

    Install or configure OJS, build workflows and prepare the journal’s public information.

  4. 04

    Testing & orientation

    Test user journeys and help the editorial team understand its operational responsibilities.

  5. 05

    Launch & development

    Support early publishing activity and identify realistic priorities for continued improvement.
