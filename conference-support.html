Conference publication

Prepare a credible and usable scholarly record

Proceedings planning should begin before submissions open. We support the organizer in defining publication criteria, collecting consistent final files and preparing publication-ready records.

  • Proceedings scope, policies and author instructions
  • Final manuscript and metadata quality checks
  • Front matter, contents, pagination and publication structure
  • ISBN or DOI workflow assistance where eligible and separately arranged
  • Online proceedings page and article-level presentation
  • Metadata export, archiving and dissemination preparation

Acceptance, DOI registration, ISBN allocation, publication and external indexing depend on the agreed publisher, platform, content quality and third-party eligibility. No indexing or citation outcome is guaranteed.
