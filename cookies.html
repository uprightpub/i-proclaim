Cookie Policy

This policy explains how cookies and related technologies may be used when you visit or interact with the i-Proclaim website.

PrivacyLegalCookies

Effective date: 3 September 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on a device by a website or an external service. Similar technologies may include local storage, pixels or server logs. They can support essential operation, remember settings, measure performance or enable external content.

2. Technologies Used on This Website

The main i-Proclaim website is a static website hosted through GitHub Pages. It does not currently provide user accounts and does not intentionally place advertising cookies of its own. Essential hosting and security systems may nevertheless process basic technical requests or use technologies needed to deliver and protect the site.

3. External Video Content

Pages may display preview images for i-Proclaim branding videos and may load a YouTube video player after you choose to play a video. When external media is requested, the provider may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details and may use cookies according to its own policies. You can avoid loading the player by not selecting the video.

4. External Links

Links may take you to journal portals, Medium, ABC Projects, reference resources or other websites. Cookies set after you leave this website are controlled by the external operator, not by i-Proclaim. You should consult the relevant provider’s cookie and privacy information.

6. Managing Cookies

You can use your browser settings to review, block or delete cookies and site data. Blocking essential technologies may affect website operation, while blocking third-party cookies may prevent external videos or other embedded services from working correctly.

7. Future Changes

If analytics, forms, consent tools or other technologies are introduced, this policy will be updated to explain their purpose. Where applicable law requires consent before a non-essential technology is used, an appropriate choice mechanism will be provided.
